## 📘 Overview
This literature review critically examines how **Large Language Models (LLMs)** such as GPT-4, PaLM, and LLaMA are transforming **E-Business**.  
It synthesises academic studies from 2018–2025, contrasting optimistic and cautious perspectives, and highlighting governance challenges in responsible AI adoption.

---

## 🧩 Key Insights
- **Applications:** Chatbots, multilingual service, marketing automation, and personalised recommendations.  
- **Opportunities:** Efficiency, cost reduction, scalability, and improved targeting.  
- **Challenges:** Bias, hallucinations, privacy, compliance, and workforce displacement.  
- **Future Directions:** Explainable AI (XAI), hybrid domain-specific models, ethical frameworks, and sustainability.
